RadiusOS platform

One active control layer across Plan, Build and Operate.

The platform does not replace RIS, PACS, LIMS, EPR, BIM, CAFM or OEM tools. It integrates around them as the commercial, operational and asset-control layer that makes each diagnostic facility repeatable.

01 / PLAN

Pre-investment digital twin

Create opportunities, model capex, annual tests, revenue, EBITDA, payback, lease yield and board-ready summary text.

02 / BUILD

Digital delivery thread

Control work packages, readiness gates, owners, statuses, blockers and practical go-live confidence.

03 / OPERATE

In-life asset intelligence

Track utilisation, uptime, monthly revenue, reporting turnaround, asset KPIs and lead requests.
